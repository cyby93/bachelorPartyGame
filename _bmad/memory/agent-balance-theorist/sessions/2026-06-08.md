# Session 2026-06-08

## Task
Balance audit after player-count scaling refactor (Saurfang). Game now supports N < 12.

## What Changed in the Codebase
- Illidan L6 `hpMult` changed from `{ base: 1.0, perPlayer: 0.10 }` to `{ base: 0.175, perPlayer: 0.175 }`
  → formula is now `hpMult = 0.175 × N` (linear, constant fight time at any N)
- `GameServer.js`: bot cap changed from hardcoded 12 → `GAME_CONFIG.MAX_PLAYERS - 1`
- `JoinScreen.svelte`: bot controls now production-visible

## Key Findings

### Illidan (L6)
- New linear formula is mathematically correct. Fight time is exactly constant at ~4:41 at all N.
- Old formula was a hidden risk: at N=4 it gave an 8:43 fight — within 3:17 of the 12-min enrage.
- Enrage buffer: 7.3 min at every player count. Ample.
- **STALE COMMENTS**: IllidanConfig.js still references `2116` constant and "8 min" target. BalanceConfig.js says "480s" and `1058` base HP. Both are wrong. Live values: `maxHp = 1200`, fight = ~4:41.

### Non-Illidan Levels — Clearance Time at N=4
- Kill objectives (enemies, bosses): ×1.47–1.65 longer at N=4 (countMult helps both sides)
- Fixed objectives (gates, buildings): ×2.13 longer at N=4 (no countMult compensation)
- All levels remain completable. L2/L3 gates/buildings will feel slow at N=4.

### Per-Player Damage Pressure at N=4
- L1/L2: ≈1.0 (neutral — high spawnMult compensates correctly)
- L5 Shade: ×1.14 (mild)
- L3 Siege: ×1.28 (notable — low spawnMult 0.04 doesn't compensate)
- L4 Leviathan: ×1.65 (most at-risk — spawnMult is 0.0, no compensation at all)

## Verdicts
- L6 Illidan: ✅ Fixed, correct
- L1 Courtyard: ✅
- L2 Gates: ✅ (slower but fine)
- L3 Siege: ⚠️ Monitor damage pressure at N=4-6
- L4 Leviathan: ⚠️ First small-group playtest priority — 65% more pressure per player
- L5 Shade: ⚠️ Protect-NPC harder at N=4 with fewer healers; pylon mechanic mitigates

## No Changes Recommended Now
All levels are completable at N=4. Tuning signals are playtest-driven, not pre-emptive.
