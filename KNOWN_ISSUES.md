## Damage and healing meter
I think currently the damage and heal recording feature works with the plain damage number that is 'dealt', not the value that is actually taken. I mean currently if I deal 60 damage to an enemy with only 40 hp, the 60 damage will be recorded instead of the actually dealt 40. This applies to the healing meter as well: If i continously heal a full healthy player with my healing abilities, all of those numbers will be added to the recorded value -> I think only the actually dealt and healed values should be recorded.
